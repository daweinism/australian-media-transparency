Fact

The News Media and Digital Platforms Mandatory Bargaining Code was enacted in February 2021 and came into effect in March 2021. It addressed bargaining power imbalances between Australian news businesses and major digital platforms.

No digital platforms were formally designated under the Code, but the framework’s existence contributed to Google and Meta entering voluntary commercial agreements with Australian news organisations.

11 — Our intervention

Talk → decision → action.

Our proposal — Not an existing government requirement.

Talk→ Decision→ Action→ Disclosure→ Verification→ Accountability

Voluntary structural disclosure

News Corp publishes the structure of major technology-platform agreements: how money is calculated, distributed, and how much reaches journalism versus overhead.

Then: independent review → public report → smaller publishers at the table.

Why this pitch

If the NBI is supposed to support a diverse news media, voluntary transparency is how a major publisher can help the public test whether that objective is being delivered.
