02 — What is a diverse media?

A healthy information ecosystem is not just large. It is varied.

Diversity is more than the number of outlets. It is whose lives, places and perspectives can still be reported.

Conceptual categories

Many kinds of news matter

First Nations, regional, local, independent, community, cultural, disability, migrant, LGBTQIA+ and socioeconomic perspectives are not interchangeable.

We do not invent statistics here. These categories frame the question of variety itself.

Variety, not volume alone

Scale can concentrate attention. Variety distributes it.

Different nodes, different positions

In a resilient ecosystem, organisations differ in size, place and purpose — and still remain audible.

Representation

Who appears in the news — and who produces it.

Multiple perspectives

Public understanding deepens when more than one vantage point can survive.

Local reporting & public interest

Democratic accountability depends on journalism that can still watch power close to home.

05 — The 2021 experiment

Travel through the bargaining era.

The News Media and Digital Platforms Mandatory Bargaining Code reshaped the settlement between platforms and publishers.

Fact

Code development

The Australian Government asked the ACCC to develop a mandatory code addressing bargaining power imbalances between Australian news media businesses and digital platforms.

Fact

Mandatory Bargaining Code enacted

The Code was enacted in February 2021 and came into effect in March 2021. It addressed bargaining power imbalances between Australian news businesses and major digital platforms.

Fact

Voluntary commercial agreements

No digital platforms were formally designated under the Code, but the existence of the framework contributed to Google and Meta entering voluntary commercial agreements with Australian news organisations.

Context

Pressure on the 2021 settlement

Commercial arrangements struck in the shadow of the Code faced sustainability questions as platform strategies and newsroom economics continued to shift.

Exact commercial terms of platform–publisher agreements were generally not public.

Policy

News Bargaining Incentive / NMI

Parliament enacted the charge–offset framework keyed to relevant Australian digital advertising revenue.

Policy design

Start with the platform

The framework applies to entities providing significant social media or internet search services in Australia.

Policy design

Relevant Australian digital advertising revenue

Liability is keyed to a revenue base that exceeds $250 million for the financial year.

Policy design

NMI liability

The enacted charge rate is 2.75% under the News Media Bargaining Charge Act 2026.

Policy design

Two pathways

Qualifying expenditure supporting registered Australian news businesses can offset liability — or the platform pays the charge.

Policy design

Guardrails for breadth

Offset entitlement requires eligible expenditure across at least 8 different news business corporate groups, with a 25% single-group contribution cap.

Important distinction

Design ≠ guaranteed outcome

The mechanism is designed to encourage support across a broader range of news businesses. It does not, by itself, guarantee diverse journalism.

Policy design

8+ news business corporate groups

To be entitled to an offset, the service group must have new eligible expenditure in relation to at least eight different news business corporate groups.

Policy design

25% single-group cap

A particular corporate group cannot account for more than one quarter of the NMI offset amount.

The policy design is intended to prevent an offset being satisfied through only a small number of news-business groups.

Our analysis

Encouragement is not a guarantee

The mechanism is designed to encourage support across a broader range of news businesses. That is not the same as proving diverse journalism outcomes.

Our proposal

Voluntary structural disclosure

News Corp voluntarily publishes the structure of its major technology-platform agreements — how money is calculated, distributed, and how much reaches journalism versus overhead.

Independent review

Disclosure is paired with independent assessment of distribution and journalism share.

Public report

Findings are published so the public can evaluate claims about diversity and sustainability.

Small publisher at the table

Bring smaller and independent publishers into the conversation about whether the system is working as claimed.

Disclosure → verification → accountability

Transparency becomes meaningful when it can be checked — and when checking can change practice.

Why this pitch

If the NBI is supposed to support a diverse news media, voluntary transparency is how a major publisher can help the public tell whether that objective is being delivered.
