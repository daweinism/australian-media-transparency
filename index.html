A visual story about Australia's News Bargaining Incentive

Key terms
News media bargaining
Negotiations between Australian news businesses and large digital platforms over payment for news content that platforms use or benefit from.
News Bargaining Incentive (NBI)
A 2026 Australian framework that charges very large digital platforms a share of their Australian digital advertising revenue. Platforms can reduce that charge by spending on Australian news under set rules, including an eight-group minimum and a 25% single-group cap.
